```text
Create a modern, luxury, high-converting 6-slide Pitch Deck for a Tech Hackathon presentation named "VIBE REWARDS - Dual-Mode Digital Membership Card System" (Starbucks Rewards Style).

DESIGN SYSTEM & AESTHETICS:
- Theme: Premium Starbucks Reserve & Modern FinTech Bento Grid style.
- Color Palette: Deep Forest Green (#006241), Luxury Champagne Gold (#D4AF37), Warm Amber (#F59E0B), Soft Off-White Background (#F7F5F0), Clean White Card Containers (#FFFFFF) with subtle drop shadows.
- Typography: Bold Modern Sans-serif (Montserrat or Plus Jakarta Sans). High visual hierarchy.
- Layout: Clean Bento Grid cards, KPI metric badges, mockups of mobile & desktop screens.

SLIDE CONTENT STRUCTURE:

Slide 1: Title & Hook
- Main Headline: VIBE REWARDS DIGITAL
- Subtitle: Dual-Mode Digital Membership System (Starbucks Rewards Architecture)
- Team: Chillandcode · Final Round · Vibe Coding Mini App 2026 (Track A - Technology)
- Key Badges: "10,000 VND = 1 Point" | "3 Dynamic Tiers" | "Camera QR Scanner (+4 Bonus Pts)"

Slide 2: Problem & The 2-Head Architecture
- Problem: Paper cards get lost, counterfeit transactions, manual point-fraud, fragmented customer experience.
- Solution: Responsive Dual-Head Web System (Toggle 1-Touch between Customer App & Cashier POS).
- Key Feature: Responsive 2-column view on Desktop (Card on Left, Rewards Catalog on Right) & 1-column stack on Mobile.

Slide 3: Head 1 - Customer Rewards Experience
- Digital Membership Card: Dynamic real-time gradient changing by tier (Bronze -> Silver -> Gold VIP).
- Star Progress Bar: Visual star milestones toward next tier.
- Digital Voucher Wallet: Real SVG Barcode (Code128) for scanner redemption at cashier counter.
- Drinks & Rewards Menu: Instant reward redemption with real-time balance deductions.

Slide 4: Head 2 - Cashier POS & Fraud Prevention
- Cashier POS Bento Grid: Fast numeric keypad, instant point converter.
- Anti-Duplicate Transaction System: Auto-generated transaction IDs (TX-KT-XXX). If an ID is re-entered, the system immediately locks and warns "Duplicate Transaction Blocked".
- Real-Time Member Mirror: Cashier sees the customer's card tier & balance update live upon submit.

Slide 5: Technical Highlights & BTC Testing Compliance
- Camera QR Scanner: Instant hardware camera scanning of member QR codes (+4 bonus points).
- BTC Reference Dataset: 1-Click "Load Sample Data" button restoring all 8 benchmark test members (Key reference member: KT-004 Dung Pham).
- 100% Vector SVG: Zero trademark/copyright risk with custom proprietary vector brand insignia.

Slide 6: Tech Stack & Production Ready
- Architecture: React 18, TypeScript Strict, Tailwind CSS, Vite, Zustand + LocalStorage (Offline-First Local Storage).
- Deployment: Instant Vercel Cloud SPA + Zero-latency local operation.
- Concluding Statement: "Vibe Rewards: Bringing Starbucks-grade loyalty technology to every retail store."
```
